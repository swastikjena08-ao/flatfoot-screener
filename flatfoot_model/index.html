Reading the heatmap

Red marks the region that pushed the model toward its answer. On a well-behaved result it sits on the hindfoot and midfoot: the calcaneus, talus and navicular. These are the bones used for clinical measures such as calcaneal pitch and Meary's angle.

If the red lands on a text marker, the film edge or empty background, treat the result with suspicion.

Limitations

  • Trained on about 540 lateral foot radiographs from a single dataset, with no external validation.
  • Labels were assigned per patient, so a normal foot of a patient with one-sided flatfoot may carry a flatfoot label.
  • Only lateral views are valid. A front view, a photo or any other image still gets an answer, and that answer is meaningless.
Model: ResNet-18 fine-tuned on lateral foot radiographs, exported to ONNX and run locally with ONNX Runtime Web. Images never leave your device.
